# Third-party sources and attribution

The MIT license covers project-owned code; third-party components retain their own licenses. Dependency versions are recorded in requirements-release.lock; their installed distributions supply license metadata.

The crop catalog contains factual identity/reference metadata attributed to GBIF name-matching and FAO/EcoCrop. Existing source URLs, source hashes, applicability restrictions and uncertainty qualifiers are retained in the two runtime catalog files. Large/raw source snapshots and observed research datasets are not part of this publication. Source facts and identifiers do not establish physiological validation or endorsement.

- GBIF: https://www.gbif.org/terms and https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/
- FAO: https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/en/
- NASA POWER: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/ ; optional operator data, not bundled observations.
- USDA NASS/ERS and Illinois farmdoc: preserved acquisition adapters reference their sources; raw market datasets/budgets are not bundled or automatically adopted by the release API.

The controlled offline fixtures and example results are repository-authored hypothetical scenarios and computed outputs. No restricted experiment files or actual private farm records are bundled.
